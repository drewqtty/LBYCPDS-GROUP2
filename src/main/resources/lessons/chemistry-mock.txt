COURSE#Chemistry for Engineers

CATEGORY#Matter and Bonding

LESSON#Atomic Structure#Atomic Structure
PAGE#Inside the Atom
An atom has a small, dense nucleus surrounded by electrons.

- Protons carry a positive charge and sit in the nucleus
- Neutrons carry no charge and also sit in the nucleus
- Electrons carry a negative charge and move around the nucleus

PAGE#Atomic Number and Mass Number
The atomic number (Z) is the number of protons. It identifies the element. In a neutral atom the number of electrons equals Z.

The mass number (A) is the number of protons plus neutrons:

```
neutrons = A - Z
```

LESSON#Chemical Bonding#Chemical Bonding
PAGE#Ionic and Covalent Bonds
Atoms bond to reach a more stable arrangement of electrons.

- Ionic bond: electrons are transferred, usually from a metal to a nonmetal
- Covalent bond: electrons are shared between nonmetal atoms

PAGE#Counting Shared Electrons
Each shared pair is one covalent bond, and a pair is 2 electrons.

- Single bond: 2 shared electrons
- Double bond: 4 shared electrons
- Triple bond: 6 shared electrons

LESSON#Stoichiometry#Stoichiometry
PAGE#Moles and Molar Mass
The mole counts particles. One mole holds about 6.022 x 10^23 particles. Molar mass is the mass of one mole, in g/mol:

```
mass = moles x molar mass
```

PAGE#Reading a Balanced Equation
The coefficients in a balanced equation give mole ratios.

```
2 H2 + O2 -> 2 H2O
```

Two moles of hydrogen react with one mole of oxygen to give two moles of water.
